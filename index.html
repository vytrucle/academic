Truc Vy Le

I am a psychology graduate from Vietnam and currently study in the Master's curriculum in Cognitive Psychology and Neuroscience at the University of Pavia, Italy. I earned my B.A. in Psychology with very good distinction from Ho Chi Minh City University of Education. My research interests focus on adverse childhood experiences (ACEs), cognitive functioning, mental health, and the neuropsychological mechanisms linking early adversity to later outcomes. I also have more than two years of clinical experience in psychological assessment and counseling.
